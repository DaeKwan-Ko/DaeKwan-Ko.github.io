Dae Kwan Ko is an Assistant Professor—Fixed Term in the MSU-DOE Plant Research Laboratory and Department of Plant Biology at Michigan State University, and an affiliate of the Great Lakes Bioenergy Research Center. He develops causal and predictive regulatory-genomics approaches to understand how cell-type-specific gene regulatory networks translate environmental change into plant adaptation. His research integrates time-resolved transcriptomics, single-nucleus multiome profiling, transcription factor–DNA binding maps, functional genetics, molecular perturbation, and interpretable modeling through a MAP → PERTURB → PREDICT framework. His work spans mechanistic studies of stress adaptation and recovery in Arabidopsis and regulatory genomics in sorghum, with the goal of identifying experimentally supported regulatory principles that predict plant responses across genotypes and environments. He is Principal Investigator on three DOE Joint Genome Institute user projects and an MSU Project GREEEN award. Ko earned his Ph.D. in Plant Biology from the University of Texas at Austin and completed postdoctoral training with C. Robin Buell and Federica Brandizzi at Michigan State University.
