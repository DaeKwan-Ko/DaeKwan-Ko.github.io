Dae Kwan Ko is an Assistant Professor—Fixed Term in the MSU-DOE Plant Research Laboratory and Department of Plant Biology at Michigan State University, and an affiliate of the Great Lakes Bioenergy Research Center. He develops causal and predictive regulatory-genomics approaches to understand how cell-type-specific gene regulatory networks shape crop responses to environmental stress. His research integrates time-resolved transcriptomics, single-nucleus multiome profiling, transcription factor–DNA binding maps, functional genetics, and interpretable artificial intelligence and machine learning to generate experimentally testable predictions and identify regulatory mechanisms and natural alleles that improve crop resilience. He has led competitive DOE Joint Genome Institute and MSU Project GREEEN projects in sorghum stress genomics. Ko earned his Ph.D. in Plant Biology from the University of Texas at Austin and completed postdoctoral training with C. Robin Buell and Federica Brandizzi at Michigan State University.
